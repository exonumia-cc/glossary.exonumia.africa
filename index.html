OUR METHOD

Bitcoin terms rarely translate
directly. So we don't just
translate them.

Terms like ‘seed phrase’ and ‘self-custody’ do not always have a direct equivalent. Native speakers propose, discuss and agree on terminology that reflects both the language and the Bitcoin concept.

文01

Propose

Native speakers propose a term that carries the real meaning — not a literal word swap.

◉02

Review

Other native speakers review and discuss the proposed terminology.

▤03

Agree

Contributors agree on the term that best communicates the concept.

◌04

Contextualise

We add definitions and usage guidance to help product teams use the term correctly.
