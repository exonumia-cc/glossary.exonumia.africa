Wallets

Wallet

noun · Software or hardware used to manage bitcoin and sign transactions.

Pochi

Programu au maunzi yanayotumika kusimamia bitcoin na kusaini miamala

Usage note

“Pochi” is the everyday Kiswahili word for a purse or wallet, so it feels familiar the moment someone opens your app.

Why it matters

Localisation is more than translation.

Bitcoin terms do not always translate directly. A literal translation might be technically correct but unfamiliar to the people using your product. The glossary gives your team reviewed terminology to use when localising your interface, onboarding, documentation and support.

Our method

Bitcoin terms rarely translate directly. So we don’t just translate them.

Terms like ‘seed phrase’ and ‘self-custody’ do not always have a direct equivalent. Native speakers propose, discuss and agree on terminology that reflects both the language and the Bitcoin concept.

01

Propose

Native speakers propose a term that carries the real meaning — not a literal word swap.

02

Review

Other native speakers review and discuss the proposed terminology.

03

Agree

Contributors agree on the term that best communicates the concept.

04

Contextualise

We add definitions and usage guidance to help product teams use the term correctly.
